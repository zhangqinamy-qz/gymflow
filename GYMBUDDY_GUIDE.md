# Welcome to GYMBUDDY

A workout tracker for logging sessions, discovering workouts, practicing sport drills, and competing with friends.

---

## Getting Started

When you first open the app, set up your profile:
- **Name** — shows on the leaderboard (must be unique within your squad)
- **Level** — Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced (affects workout recommendations)
- **Equipment** — what you have access to (affects which workouts flag as missing gear)

---

## Main Features

### Home
Your dashboard. Shows:
- **This Week / All Time / My Stars** — tap any card to go to your full history
- Quick buttons to Browse or Create
- Squad leaderboard (see below)
- Recommended workouts based on your level

### Browse — Workouts & Drills
Two modes, toggled at the top:

**Workouts** — preset library filtered by category (Running, Ball Sports, Strength) and difficulty. Tap a workout to see the full exercise list with muscle diagrams and YouTube form videos. Workouts marked "Missing equipment" need gear you haven't listed in your profile.

**Drills** — sport-specific skill drills for Volleyball and Pickleball. Filter by sport then by skill (Serving, Passing, Spiking, Dinking, etc.). Each drill card expands to show the full description and difficulty.

### Start a Workout
From any workout page, hit **START WORKOUT**. The app guides you step by step through:
1. Warm-up
2. Main exercises
3. Cool-down

When you finish, log your time and any extras (calories, distance, heart rate).

**Stars earned:**
- 1★ — 30 min session
- 2★ — 45 min session
- 3★ — 60+ min session ("LEGENDARY!")

### History
All past sessions on a calendar. Tap any stat card on the Home page to get here directly.

### Create Workout
Build a custom workout:
1. Pick exercises from the library (or type a custom name)
2. Set sets × reps or duration — ball sport games (Volleyball, Pickleball, Tennis) skip this step automatically and show "Full game"
3. Hit **Auto-Generate Warm-Up & Cool-Down** — the app recommends stretches based on the muscles you're working
4. Save — your workout appears in Browse

## Squad — Compete with Friends

Squad members share a real-time leaderboard and their workout history syncs across devices.

**To create a squad (first person):**
1. Go to Home → scroll to the **SQUAD** section
2. Tap **+ Create New Squad**
3. Share the 6-character code with your friends

**To join a squad:**
1. Home → SQUAD section → enter the code → **JOIN**

**Name rules:** Each name must be unique within a squad. If your name is already taken, you'll need to change it in your profile before joining.

**Cross-device sync:** Once in a squad, workouts you log are stored in the cloud. Open the app on another device with the same name and squad code, and your history will sync automatically. The leaderboard updates in real time — most stars wins.

## Tips
- Tap the stat cards on Home to jump straight to your history
- Edit any preset workout to add or remove exercises (trash icon while editing)
- Mark exercises 👎 to exclude them from future recommendations
- Ball sport games (Volleyball / Pickleball / Tennis) can be added to any custom workout — no sets or reps needed
- Switch light/dark mode with the toggle in the nav bar
